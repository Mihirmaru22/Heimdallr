Croatia adopted the euro as its currency on 1 January 2023, becoming the 20th member state of the eurozone. [3] A fixed conversion rate was set at €1 = 7.5345 kn. [4] Croatia's previous currency, the kuna (Croatian for marten), had used the euro as its main reference since its creation in 1994.
