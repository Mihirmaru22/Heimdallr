A Bureaucrat's Misread Memo Accidentally Opened the Berlin Wall in 1989 On November 9, 1989, a fumbling East German spokesman named Günter Schabowski misread a memo on live TV and accidentally declared the Berlin Wall open — while Gorbachev learned what happened from the evening news.
