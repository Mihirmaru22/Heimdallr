Mistral AI SAS (French: [mistʁal]) is a French artificial intelligence (AI) company headquartered in Paris. Founded in 2023, it develops large language models (LLMs). As of September 2026, the company has a valuation of US$24.7 billion, the highest among European AI companies. [2]
