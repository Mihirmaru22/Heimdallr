TITLE: Croatia introduces the euro - European Central Bank
DATE: 2026-10-13T05:38:42.339200+00:00

Jan 1, 2023 · With Croatia joining the euro area, Hrvatska narodna banka, the country’s national central bank, becomes a member of the Eurosystem. The euro area’s central banking system comprises the ECB and the national central banks of those countries whose currency is the euro.
