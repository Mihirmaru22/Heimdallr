May 15, 2026 · The International Space Station (ISS) is a marvel of global cooperation, involving five major space agencies from six nations: the United States (NASA), Russia (Roscosmos), Japan (JAXA), Canada (CSA), and the European Union (ESA). Together, they represent a unified effort to advance space science, technology, and exploration.
