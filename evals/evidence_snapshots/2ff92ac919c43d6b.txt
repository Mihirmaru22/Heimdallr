TITLE: Who owns GitHub? Parent company, trademark & evidence (2026)
DATE: 2026-10-13T05:35:00.525821+00:00

GitHub is owned by Microsoft (NASDAQ: MSFT). Microsoft acquired GitHub, Inc. in 2018 for US$7.5 billion in Microsoft stock (announced June, closed October 2018). GitHub keeps its own brand and operates as a Microsoft subsidiary; its trademarks are held by GitHub, Inc. Ownership is rarely one hop.
