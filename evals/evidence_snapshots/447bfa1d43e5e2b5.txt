TITLE: The James Webb Telescope Lights Up the Sky During Launch
DATE: 2026-10-13T05:35:33.337247+00:00

Aug 29, 2023 · NASA’s James Webb Space Telescope, or JWST, launched aboard Arianespace’s Ariane 5 rocket on Saturday, Dec. 25, 2021, from the ELA-3 Launch Zone of Europe’s Spaceport at the Guiana Space Centre in Kourou, French Guiana.
