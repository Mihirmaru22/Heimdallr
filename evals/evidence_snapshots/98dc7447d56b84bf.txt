Anthropic CEO and Leadership FAQ Who is Dario Amodei? Dario Amodei is Anthropic's co-founder and chief executive. He holds a PhD in biophysics from Princeton, worked at Baidu and Google Brain, and was Vice President of Research at OpenAI, where he led the teams behind GPT-2 and GPT-3.
