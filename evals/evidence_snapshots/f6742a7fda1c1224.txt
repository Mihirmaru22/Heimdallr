NASA's James Webb Space Telescope launched at 7:20 a.m. EST on December 25, 2021 on an Ariane 5 rocket from Europe's Spaceport in French Guiana, South America. A camera fixed on the launch vehicle's upper stage captured Webb's separation from the launch vehicle at 7:47 a.m. EST.
