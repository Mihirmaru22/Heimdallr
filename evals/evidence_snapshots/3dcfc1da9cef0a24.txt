TITLE: Croatia adopts the euro - European Central Bank
DATE: 2026-10-13T05:38:46.641943+00:00

On 12 July 2022 the Council of the European Union formally approved Croatia’s accession to the euro area and determined a Croatian kuna conversion rate of 7.53450 per euro.
