TITLE: What Watson and Crick Did: The DNA Double Helix
DATE: 2026-10-13T05:36:39.667418+00:00

Sep 24, 2026 · In April 1953, James Watson and Francis Crick published a paper in Nature proposing that DNA has a double-helix structure: two spiraling chains of sugar and phosphate running in opposite directions, held together by paired chemical bases on the inside. The model did more than describe a shape.
