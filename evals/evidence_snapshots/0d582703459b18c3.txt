Mar 25, 2026 · Anthropic, Inc., a Stock Corporation, is a business entity located in San Francisco, CA. Officially filed on February 3, 2021, this corporation is recognized under the document number 4688086. Governed by the California Secretary of State, the corporation maintains an active filing status. Anthropic operates as a Delaware public benefit corporation legally obligated to balance the interests of stockholders with its public benefit purpose of developing AI systems that are reliable, interpretable, steerable, and beneficial for humanity over the long term. Anthropic's main headquarters is situated at 548 Market St in San Francisco, California. This location functions as the primary base of operations for the AI safety and research company. Anthropic is an American artificial intelligence company founded in 2021 by former OpenAI executives Dario and Daniela Amodei. The San Francisco-based public benefit corporation develops Claude, a leading family of large language models.
