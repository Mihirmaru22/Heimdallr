TITLE: Croatia (since 1 January 2023) - European Central Bank
DATE: 2026-10-13T05:38:42.338134+00:00

On 12 July 2022 the Council of the European Union approved the accession of Croatia to the euro area on 1 January 2023 and determined the conversion rate for the Croatian kuna. Prices for goods and services will be indicated in both euro and kuna until 31 December 2023.
