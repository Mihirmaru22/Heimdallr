TITLE: It's official: IAU demotes Pluto | Astronomy.com
DATE: 2026-10-13T06:40:12.123394+00:00

IAU/Martin Kornmesser August 24, 2006; updated August 25 Astronomers meeting in Prague have decided by a wide margin that Pluto no longer merits consideration as a full-fledged planet.
