The unit of amount of substance in the SI is the mole (symbol: mol), a base unit. [1] Since 2019, the mole has been defined such that the value of the Avogadro constant NA is exactly 6.022 140 76 × 1023 mol−1, defining a macroscopic unit convenient for use in laboratory-scale chemistry.
