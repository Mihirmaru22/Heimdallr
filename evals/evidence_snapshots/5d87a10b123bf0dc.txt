TITLE: Croatia to join euro area | Deutsche Bundesbank
DATE: 2026-10-13T05:38:42.340746+00:00

Dec 27, 2022 · Croatia is introducing euro cash on 1 January 2023, making it the 20th Member State of the euro area. Croatian kuna banknotes can be exchanged for euro free of charge at all Bundesbank branches between 1 January and 28 February 2023.
