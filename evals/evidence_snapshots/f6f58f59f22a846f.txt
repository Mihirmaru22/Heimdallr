Aug 25, 2026 · What Is the Speed of Light? The speed of light in a vacuum is exactly 299,792,458 metres per second. Not approximately — exactly. Since 1983, this number has been locked into the foundations of the metric system itself: one metre is now defined as the distance light covers in precisely 1/299,792,458 of a second.
