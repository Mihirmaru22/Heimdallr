Aug 4, 2026 · Microsoft owns GitHub. The acquisition was announced in June 2018 and closed that October, valuing GitHub at $7.5 billion in Microsoft stock. GitHub had spent its first decade as an independent company, founded in 2008 and venture-funded before the sale.
