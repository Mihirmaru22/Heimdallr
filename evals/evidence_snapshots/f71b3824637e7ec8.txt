You need to add this to the top of the script: Start-Service -Name 'Dhcp' route add 169.254.169.254 MASK 255.255.255.255 0.0.0.0 The DHCP Client is stopped when the script is triggered. That why you don't have IP. And you also need to add a route to the meta-data service. Sysprep remove those routes.
