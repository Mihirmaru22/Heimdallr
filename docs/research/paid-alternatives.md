# WebData AI: paid alternatives to consider later (research only, not for the MVP)

Prepared 2026-10-19. All "verified" prices were read with WebFetch on 2026-10-19 from the vendor's own pricing page or docs, linked in the last column. Anything marked **unverified** could not be confirmed on a vendor page. Prices change often, so check them again before buying anything.

## Workload model (used in every estimate)

- **Discovery:** about 50 search queries (search APIs), or 500 company lookups (company-data providers).
- **Crawling:** 500 companies x 8 pages = **4,000 page fetches**. About 10% need JS rendering: **400 JS pages + 3,600 static pages**.
- **Paid LLMs are out of scope.** Field extraction is assumed to happen in our own pipeline. Vendor-side extraction add-ons are listed only for reference.
- Estimates are one-off costs for one 500-company job. "Free allowance covers it" means $0 at this volume. A **monthly minimum dominates** when the cheapest plan that covers the job costs much more than the metered usage.

## 2. Overall ranking: top 5 by value and fit for WebData AI

1. **Parallel (FindAll + Search + Extract).** FindAll is the only priced product that does "find N companies matching criteria" directly: about $15.25 for 500 matches at Base. Search and Extract are each $1–5 per 1k, and the free allowance is large.
2. **Firecrawl.** One vendor covers search, crawl, JS rendering and markdown. $19/mo Hobby covers the whole 4,100-credit job, and it's the easiest drop-in replacement for our crawler.
3. **Exa.** Semantic search that is good at finding company lists. Search plus contents for this job is about $4.35, all inside the monthly free credits.
4. **Zyte API.** The cheapest robust crawling with no subscription ($0.87 to $3.19 for 4,000 pages). It charges only for successful responses and handles anti-bot protection. A good fallback for sites our crawler fails on.
5. **Diffbot (Knowledge Graph + Extract).** Structured organization entities (homepage, HQ, industries) plus automatic extraction. The free plan covers the crawl; KG enrichment for 500 companies needs Startup at $299, or two free months.

Honorable mentions: **Brave Search API** (independent index, $5 monthly credit covers about 1,000 searches, simple prepaid billing), **Tavily** (search and crawl in about 850 credits, inside the free tier), **People Data Labs** ($100 Pro minimum, clean firmographics) and **Bright Data** (5,000 free unblocked requests/mo).

Not recommended for this product: Google CSE (closed to new customers, shuts down 2027-01-01), Clearbit (no standalone sales), OpenCorporates (no website, email or category fields), and Crunchbase, PitchBook and Dealroom (enterprise quotes and startup-only scope; would need licensing review).

## 3. Approval notes and what to integrate first

**Would need explicit approval before any use:**
- **Any recurring subscription**, even small ones (Firecrawl $19, ScrapingBee $19, Coresignal $199, Diffbot $299, PDL $100, The Companies API $95). For most of these, the monthly minimum dominates at 500 companies/month.
- **Enterprise or contract vendors**: Crunchbase, PitchBook, Dealroom, Apollo (API only on the Custom plan), OpenCorporates and BuiltWith. Prices aren't published or come with heavy minimums, and their **data licences need legal review before data goes into Excel files we hand to customers** (redistribution, attribution, resale).
- **Unblocking and proxy services** (Bright Data, Zyte, ScrapingBee premium/stealth, ScraperAPI): legal/ToS review for bypassing site anti-bot protection, plus robots.txt policy.
- **Contact emails of named people** (Apollo, PDL person data, Coresignal's LinkedIn-derived data): personal data under GDPR, since the target is European companies. Prefer generic role addresses (info@, sales@) found on the companies' own sites.
- **Unverified items**: the ownership of the Crawl4AI cloud gate, Serper's pack pricing, Jina's paid token prices, ScraperAPI's JS surcharge and The Companies API per-call credits. Confirm all of these with the vendor before committing.

**Integration order if a budget appears:**
1. **Parallel**, pay-as-you-go: put FindAll behind the discovery step (one query returns about 500 candidate companies for about $15), with Search as the cheap fallback. No subscription.
2. **Exa or Brave**: a second discovery source to improve recall. Both are metered with monthly free credits, so usage stays at about $0.
3. **Zyte API** in PAYG mode, used only as a fallback when our self-hosted crawler is blocked or a page needs JS. This keeps spend in the low single dollars per job with no commitment.
4. **Firecrawl Hobby ($19/mo)** if we'd rather replace self-hosted crawling entirely than maintain it.
5. Only later, if customers need firmographics we can't get from websites (headcount, funding): **PDL Pro** or **Diffbot KG**, after the licensing review above.
