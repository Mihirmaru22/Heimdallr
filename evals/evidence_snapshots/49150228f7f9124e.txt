TITLE: The Pluto System After New Horizons - JSTOR
DATE: 2026-10-13T05:37:42.482016+00:00

Pluto is one of a half dozen worlds in the solar system known to have evolving surface-atmosphere-mediated climates. Climate and climate evolution are of intense interest because of their implications for planetary habitability, surface activity, and fate.
