Sep 28, 2026 · What does Google DeepMind do? Google DeepMind is an artificial intelligence research lab that builds AI models and systems, including the Gemini family of models used across Google's products. It is funded by its parent company Alphabet, and its research feeds into Google's consumer, cloud and developer services rather than being sold separately.
