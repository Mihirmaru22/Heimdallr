The speed of light, denoted by the letter c, is the speed at which electromagnetic radiation — including visible light, radio waves, X-rays, and gamma rays — travels through a vacuum. It is the speed at which all electromagnetic radiation travels in vacuum. The numerical value of the speed of light in vacuum, symbol c, is defined to be exactly 299 792 458 when expressed in the unit metre per second, m s −1.
