TITLE: On the Origin of the Pluto System - arXiv.org
DATE: 2026-10-13T05:37:42.480942+00:00

We focus on the giant impact hypothesis currently understood as the likeliest origin for the Pluto-Charon binary, and devote particular attention to new models of planet formation and migration in the outer solar system. We discuss the origins conundrum posed by the system's four small moons.
