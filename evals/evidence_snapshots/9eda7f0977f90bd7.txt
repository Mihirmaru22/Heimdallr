TITLE: Leibniz–Newton calculus controversy explained
DATE: 2026-10-13T05:37:51.386255+00:00

In the history of calculus, the calculus controversy (de|Prioritätsstreit|lit=priority dispute) was an argument between mathematicians Isaac Newton and Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz over who had first invented calculus.
